\begin{frame}
OP'MOTION'ALIGN'OBS'PRIORITY
\begin{verbatim}
     FUNCTION:

     THE OPAAPRY SUBFUNCTION SHALL DETERMINE THE OBSERVATION TO
     BE USED IN THE NEXT KALMAN CYCLE, DURING MOTION ALIGNMENT.
     DURING COARSE ALIGNMENT, EITHER GPS, TRANSFER, OR DVS
     VELOCITY OBSERVATIONS SHALL BE TAKEN.  DURING FINE
     ALIGNMENT EITHER GPS-POSITION, TRANSFER-POSITION, OR
     DVS-VELOCITY OBSERVATIONS SHALL BE CHOSEN, WITH DISCRETE
     POSITION FIXES GIVEN HIGHEST PRIORITY.  THE SAME
     CONTINOUS OBSERVATION SHALL BE CHOSEN FOR EACH KALMAN
     CYCLE UNTIL IT IS UNAVAILABLE FOR THREE CYCLES.  DURING
     AUTO MODE, OPAAPRY WILL AUTOMATICALLY CYCLE AMONG THE
     AVAILABLE AIDS.  DURING FINE ALIGNMENT, BARO OBSERVATIONS
     SHALL BE TAKEN EVERY TENTH KALMAN CYCLE, IF AVAILABLE.

\end{verbatim}
\end{frame}
