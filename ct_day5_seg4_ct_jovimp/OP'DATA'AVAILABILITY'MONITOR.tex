OP'DATA'AVAILABILITY'MONITOR
\begin{verbatim}
     FUNCTION: The DATAVL subfunction shall monitor the availabilty of
     observation for the operational Kalman filters. DATAVL shall
     monitor all observations at all times regardless of whether the
     presently operating filters can utilize the observations.  DATAVL
     shall output indications of available observations in terms of 
     DATAVL1 & DATAVL2 the format of which is in figure below this
     paragraph.  The processing must assure that data is continuously
     available regardless if the present operational filters can 
     utilize the observation at the very start of the Kalman cycle.
     The availability criterion shall be: at least 
     (DPLRAT * DELTA'T'KALMAN)/4 pieces of data were present during
     the last Kalman cycle.  If this is true, OPDATAVL, logical flags
     shall be set equal to .TRUE. for the next Kalman cycle.  (Note:
     OPDATAVL is\ zeroed in KLBEGIN each Kalman cycle.)
