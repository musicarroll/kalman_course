OP'OBSERVATION'PROCESSING
\begin{verbatim}
     FUNCTION: The observation processing  subfunction shall choose the
     best observation for the operational filters.  OBPRC shall choose
     the same observation for the INU's if both are in aided nav or
     fin  air align.  If an INU is in coarse air align the best
     available velocity observation shall be chosen for that INU.
     During fine air align all available aids shall be chosen from
     those consistent with the alignment type (TAS, GPS, Transfer or
     DVS).  DIscrete aids shall always be chosen if available (the
     operator must see th response to requested fix).  The diescrete
     aids shall include postion fixes and radar altitude calibrations.

     The "available" observation shall be dependent or the system Nav
     Mode for Aided Naviagtion an the Motion align type for motion
     align.

     Observation processing shall not preform BITE processing on data
     availablility but rather shall respond to changes in mode from NAV
     mode control.  OPOBPRC shall coutn data availability (as a
     functionof mode) and make the information available to Nav mode
     control, which will perform  the requisite mode changes.  For each
     mode, OPOBPRC shall monitor availability of the "prime" aid.
     these prime modes are noted by atserisks (*) in the aid table.

     The CT acumulators shall be reset when a new observation is
     chosen.  The time differences between  the valid Navigation data
     and observation data shall be used to time synchronize the
     Navigation data with the observation data.  The synchronization
     shall be sent to the Kalman filter for observation processing.

     Once an observation is deselected in favor of another, it shall
     not be relsected in the same Kalman cycle.
