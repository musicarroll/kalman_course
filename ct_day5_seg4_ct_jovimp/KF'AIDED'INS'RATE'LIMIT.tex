KF'AIDED'INS'RATE'LIMIT
\begin{verbatim}
     FUNCTION: The KFCOVINS functions calculates covariance rate limiting
     for the aided inertial navaigation Kalman filter.  KFCOVINS makes use
     of the INS error budget information provided via the INS standardized
     interface.  The unmodelled states accounted for in KFCOVINS include:

               o Gyro Drift Randomness
               o Gyro Warmup
               o Gyro Scale Factor
               o Gyro Misalignment
	       o Gyro Trending
               o Gyro Mass Unbalance
               o Accelerometer Randomness
               o Accelerometer Scale Factor
               o Accelerometer Misalignment
               o Accelerometer Warmup
               o Gravity Anomalies

     KFCOVINS shall use an alogorithm appropriate to both air alignment
     and aided navigation and for both strapdown and gimballed INUs.
     The same algorithm shall be used for coarse air align, fine air
     align, and aided navigation.

     IN THIS ALGORITHM, THETA SUBSCRIPT I IS THE ACCUMULATED GYRO

\end{verbatim}
