\begin{frame}
KF'COV'EXTRAPOLATION
\begin{verbatim}
     FUNCTION:

       THIS FUNCTIONS EXTRAPOLATES THE COVARIANCE MATRIX BETWEEN
       MEASUREMENT UPDATES USING A U-D FACTORED FORMULATION.
       THE EQUATION IS:

             P  = F P FT + Z

       WHERE F IS THE TRANSITION MATRIX BETWEEN UPDATES, Z IS
       THE COVARIANCE RATE LIMITING (SYSTEM NOISE) MATRIX AND
       P IS THE EXTRAPOLATED COVARIANCE MATRIX.

       THE COVARIANCE MATRIX IS EXTRAPOLATED IN U-D FACTORED
       FORM. THE U-D FACTORS ARE STORED IN VECTOR FORM IN THE
       TABLE IN'UK WITH THE ELEMENTS OF D OVERLAYING THE 1"S
       ON THE U DIAGONAL. THE ROUTINE FIRST PERFORMS THE
       MULTIPLICATION OF F AND U IN THE EXPRESSION:

             (FU) D (FU) TRANSPOSE

       THE SPARSE MULTIPLICATION ROUTINE IS CALLED TO PERFORM
       THIS MULTIPLICATION. THE ROUTINE NEXT PERFORMS A MODIFIED
       GRAM SCHMIDT ORTHOGONALIZATION PROCESS TO OBTAIN
       EXTRAPOLATED U-D FACTORS. THIS METHOD INTRINSICALLY
       PERFORMS THE FU D FU TRANSPOSE MULTIPLICATION WHILE
       SUMMING IN THE Z MATRIX AND EXTRACTING THE NEW U-D
       FACTORS.



\end{verbatim}
\end{frame}
