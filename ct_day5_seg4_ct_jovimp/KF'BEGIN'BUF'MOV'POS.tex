\begin{verbatim}
     FUNCTION:  This routine buffers observation data to Kalman filter processing.  
                This data includes residual tilts for motion align, vertical 
		loop feedback constants, and H matrix sums.  It also compensates
		integrated velocity observations for data dropouts at the end of
		cycles and monitors the status of motion alignment.
								   
\end{verbatim}
