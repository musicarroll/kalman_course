\begin{frame}
OP'RESET'CT'INS'ACCUMULATORS
\begin{verbatim}
     FUNCTION: THE OPCTRSTI SUBFUNCTION SHALL RESET THE 4 HZ CT DATA
               ACCUMULATORS TO ZERO.  OPCTRSTI SHALL BE CALLED BY BEGIN
               AT THE START OF EACH KALMAN CYCLE AFTER THE CALL TO
               OPPTRSTI.  OPCTRSTI SHALL ALSO BE CALLED WHENEVER A NEW
               OBSERVATION IS SELECTED DURING THE COURSE OF ONE KALMAN
               CYCLE (FROM OBPRC).

\end{verbatim}
\end{frame}
