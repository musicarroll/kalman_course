KF'DOPPLER'OBS'NOISE
\begin{verbatim}
     FUNCTION:
             THIS FUNCTION PERFORMS CALCULATES THE INCREMENTAL
             NOISE MATRIX FOR A DOPPLER OBSERVATION DUE
             TO UNMODELLED STATES. THE OUTPUT IS VALID AT
             THE TIME OF THE OBSERVATION.

\end{verbatim}
