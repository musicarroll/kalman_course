\begin{frame}
KF'KALMAN'DATA'BUFFERING
\begin{verbatim}
     FUNCTION:

     THE KFBEGIN SUBFUNCTION SHALL PERFORM THE NECESSARY DATA
     BUFFERING FROM FASTER CYCLES:

        o BUFFER FROM 4 HZ WHERE THE TRANSITION MATRIX IS
          ACCUMULATED, POSITION FIX UPDATES ARE COMPUTED,
          AND NAV MODE FLAGS ARE SET.

        o BUFFER FROM 1 HZ WHERE THE EARTH RADII
          COMPUTATIONS ARE DONE.

     THE REQUIRED VARIABLE ASSOCIATIONS SHALL BE MADE WITH
     INTERRUPTS DISABLED.

     KFBEGIN SHALL ALSO PERFORM FILTER INITIALIZATIONS BY
     CALLING KFKALINT.


\end{verbatim}
\end{frame}
