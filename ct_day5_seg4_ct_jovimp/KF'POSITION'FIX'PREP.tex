\begin{frame}
KF'POSITION'FIX'PREP
\begin{verbatim}
     FUNCTION:

          THIS ROUTINE CALCULATES THE GAINS AND NOISE REQUIRED FOR
          THE CDU DISPLAY DURING POSITION FIXES. THESE INCLUDE THE
          TWO GAIN VECTORS (ONE FOR THE X AND ONE FOR THE Y RESIDUALS),
          THE INNOVATIONS FOR THE X AND THE Y RESIDUALS, AND DISPLAY
          OF THE QUALITY RATIO.

          THE OUPUTS ARE:

               X NORMALIZED GAIN
               Y NORMALIZED GAIN
               Z NORMALIZED GAIN
               X INNOVATION, ZERO NOISE
               Y INNOVATION, ZERO NOISE
               Z INNOVATION, ZERO NOISE

          THE POSITION UPDATE PROCESSING SUPPLIES NOISE AS A FUNCTION
          OF THE RANGE AND ALTITUDE OF THE FIX.


\end{verbatim}
\end{frame}
