\begin{frame}
KF'OBSERVATION'CHECK
\begin{verbatim}
     FUNCTION:

              THIS FUNCTION PERFORMS A 'GOODNESS' CHECK ON THE
              OBSERVATIONS AFTER MEASUREMENT UPDATE PROCESSING.
              THE OBSERVATION RESIDUAL IS COMPARED TO THE
              INNOVATION AND TO AN OBSOLUTE LIMIT WHICH
              DETERMINES THE MAXIMUM POSITION UPDATE WHICH MAY
              BE MADE TO THE NAVIGATION EQUATIONS.

              THIS ROUTINE PERFORMS OBSERVATION REJECTION
              CHECKING. AN INDICATION OF THE REJECTION OF AN
              OBSERVATION THREE TIMES IS SET BY THE ROUTINE.
              SEPERATE INDICATION ARE MAINTAINED FOR EACH INU,
              AND FOR THE DVS FILTER.

              IF AN UNREASONABLE POSITION OR ALTITUDE FIX IS
              PROCESSED THIS ROUTINE IS EXITED.

\end{verbatim}
\end{frame}
