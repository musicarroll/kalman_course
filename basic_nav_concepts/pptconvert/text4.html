Navigation Aids

  • Position Fix

    • Visual flyover or driveover
    • Radar position fix
      • Cursor on radar screen initially placed over where the nav computer thinks the target is
      • Operator manually corrects by moving cursor to actual target
      • Nav computer incorporates deltas as offset corrections
    • Altitude Calibration
      • Over terrain point of known elevation
      • Use radar altimeter to measure distance from aircraft to ground
      • Correct for errors in baroaltimeter and INS vertical channel
