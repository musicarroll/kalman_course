Navigation Aids

  • Radio Navigation

    • VOR: VHF Omnidirectional Range sensor
      • Receiver senses direction of signal (the radial) from external source and computes distance and bearing to external source location
        • Intersection of two radials yields a position fix
      • DME: Distance Measuring Equipment
      • TACAN: Tactical Air Navigation = VOR + DME for military (more accurate)
    • LORAN-C: Long Range Navigation
      • Time difference of arrival from two sources
        • Yields hyperbolic lines of position
      • Third source needed to intersect the hyperbolas
      • One master station and two secondary stations
