Navigation Aids

  • Satellite Navigation

    • Global Positioning System (GPS)
      • Six orbital planes, inclined at 55 degrees
      • Four or more satellites per orbital plane
      • Atomic clocks time tag signals transmitted
      • User receivers determine time of flight of signal and computer pseudorange: R=cΔT to each of the visible satellites
      • At least four must be visible to determine position and compensate for receiver clock bias
